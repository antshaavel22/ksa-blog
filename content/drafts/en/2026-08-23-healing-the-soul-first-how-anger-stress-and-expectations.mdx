Sergey Lazarev: His Personal Journey and Healing a Spinal Hernia

Any true, lasting healing of physical symptoms must begin with the soul, as the soul is the energetic foundation upon which our physical body is built. This is the core message of Sergey Nikolaevich Lazarev, a prominent writer, philosopher, and researcher of spiritual laws.

The power of Lazarev's system lies in the fact that his teachings are not dry academic formulas. Instead, they are forged in his own intense, life-changing experiences—none more famous than the story of how he healed his own severe spinal hernia in just three days.

## The Spinal Hernia and the Rigid Mind

While on a trip abroad, Lazarev and his wife agreed to meet at a supermarket. His wife arrived thirty minutes late. As he stood waiting, he felt a wave of intense irritation and anger rising within him. He was trapped in the intellectual concept of "justice"—the rigid, mental demand that she must be punctual and respect his time.

Shortly after this emotional outburst, he attempted to lift a heavy boat engine with one hand and injured his back, resulting in an agonizing, paralyzing spinal hernia that made it impossible to walk or lie flat.

Instead of seeking medical intervention, Lazarev paused and looked deep inward. He realized that spinal hernias are deeply connected to subconscious resentment toward women and a rigid, intellectual demand that reality must conform exactly to our plans. He began to consciously forgive his wife, surrender his rigid expectations, and accept the situation as something organized by a higher, wiser divine order.

Within three days of this internal shift, the pain completely vanished, and the hernia resolved without a trace. This experience taught him that physical pain is merely a loud alarm indicating that our analytical intellect has displaced unconditional love from our soul.

## The Self-Destruction Program and the Danger of Low Self-Esteem

Unresolved negative emotions and deep resentments do not simply fade away; they sink in layers into our subconscious. If we do not actively clear them through love and forgiveness, they can activate what Lazarev calls a "program of self-destruction".

Lazarev recounts the case of a female patient who experienced a highly painful, bitter breakup. Stricken with low self-esteem, deep self-dissatisfaction, and anger at her destiny, she unconsciously directed her aggressive energy inward. This internal aggression quickly manifested physically as a uterine myoma, followed by severe, life-threatening, and uncontrollable bleeding.

When she called Lazarev in deep distress, he gave her a direct spiritual diagnosis: "Your program of self-destruction is highly active because you are completely dissatisfied with yourself and your fate. You must stop these destructive thoughts, forgive, and pray." She immediately changed her inner state, redirecting her energy toward unconditional love, forgiveness, and acceptance of her path. The bleeding stopped completely the very next day.

## The Energy Crisis: How Anxiety and Control Kill Us

How do we trigger these severe physical illnesses? Lazarev explains that our analytical intellect is obsessed with time and control.

**Aggression Against Time.** When we constantly worry about the future, regret the past, or harbor irritation toward the present, we commit what he calls an "aggression against time".

**Energetic Bankruptcy.** This relentless mental planning and worrying drains an enormous amount of our vital life force. It leaves our spiritual-energetic body (our aura) completely bankrupt, which eventually manifests on the physical plane as chronic and severe disease.

To initiate healing, we must practice "restricting the spirit"—silencing our future-obsessed intellect and anchoring our awareness strictly in the "here and now". Lazarev highlights two powerful physical practices that force this hyperactive mind to quiet down:

**Radical Silence.** Committing to complete silence for more than six days completely shuts down the analytical intellect, releasing massive reservoirs of trapped mental energy for deep self-healing.

**Breath Restriction (The Buteyko Method).** Consciously limiting your breathing reduces oxygen intake. While science attributes its benefits to carbon dioxide levels, Lazarev explains that by depriving the physical body of external physical energy (oxygen), the system is forced to make an energetic "switch," drawing instead on deep, internal spiritual energy received through the soul.
