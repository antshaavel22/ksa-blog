Have you ever accidentally scratched your eye? Maybe a young child reached out to touch your face and swiped your eye with a fingernail, or a stray branch caught you during a walk. The pain is intense and immediate! Your eye waters, lights hurt, and keeping it open feels almost impossible.

## Your Eye's Built-In Healing Power

But here is the good news: the very front of your eye has a built-in superhero capability. The outermost clear layer of your eye, called the corneal epithelium, is one of the fastest-healing tissues in the entire human body.

## How Limbal Stem Cells Work

It relies on a special cellular "factory" at the edge of the cornea called limbal stem cells. These cells constantly create fresh, new cells that march toward the center of your eye to keep the surface perfectly smooth and transparent.

Because of this rapid biological clock, a simple superficial scratch can heal completely in just a couple of days!

## Modern Laser Vision Correction and Natural Healing

At KSA Silmakeskus, we are deeply inspired by this natural self-healing design. In fact, our modern, touch-free laser vision correction procedures are designed to work with this biological clock.

Instead of making permanent structural cuts into the deeper layers of your cornea, the laser gently reshapes the surface, allowing your eye's natural healing shield to rebuild itself cleanly, safely, and naturally.

## When to Seek Professional Care

**What to remember:** Your corneal surface is built to repair itself rapidly, but a painful scratch should always be examined by an eye care professional to ensure there are no hidden foreign bodies or risks of infection.
