We live in an era where we are constantly bombarded with expert advice and rules on what, when, and how much to eat. Yet, despite all our access to nutritional science, chronic diseases, metabolic fatigue, and sensory numbness have never been more widespread. How is it that wild animals navigate their well-being with effortless, instinctive precision, while we, armed with all our intellect, live in a state of constant physical struggle?

## Healing Begins with the Soul

To truly understand this paradox, we must look to the spiritual-energetic framework developed by Sergey Nikolaevich Lazarev. Lazarev is a prominent writer, philosopher, and researcher of spiritual laws who has spent decades exploring the deep, invisible connections between a person's physical health, their emotional state, and their ultimate destiny. The core postulate of Lazarev's philosophy is both simple and profound: all physical illnesses and bodily ailments are merely the final, outward manifestation of unresolved emotional blockages, deep-seated resentments, and a fundamental neglect of the soul.

Lazarev is far from an academic theorist. He arrived at his conclusions through intense personal tests and real-world trials. In one of the most remarkable chapters of his life, he walked away from a highly successful, high-paying career and a comfortable lifestyle in the warm resort city of Sochi. Following a persistent, illogical subconscious "inner pull," he spent five years working under harsh, physically demanding, and risky conditions on a construction site in St. Petersburg.

This experience proved to him that our subconscious feelings are always wiser and far more protective than the calculating, logical plans of our intellect. In Lazarev's healing system, the absolute first step toward physical recovery is re-establishing our internal spiritual hierarchy—prioritizing unconditional love and faith over the demanding, control-seeking ego. When our soul is clean and aligned, our biological intuition naturally sharpens, guiding us effortlessly toward life-preserving physical choices.

## Instinct versus Intellect

To illustrate just how far modern humans have strayed from this natural, biological intuition, Lazarev shares a fascinating observation of squirrels in a city park. The squirrels were presented with two piles of food: genetically modified (GMO) corn and regular, organic corn.

The result was stark: the squirrels barely nibbled at the GMO corn, rejecting it almost instantly, but they ate the natural corn completely down to the very last kernel.

How did these wild creatures know? They did not consult a nutritionist, read scientific literature, or analyze chemical compositions. They relied entirely on their built-in biological compass: their senses of smell, taste, and touch. Their instinctual feelings immediately warned them that the GMO option was a danger, prompting them to turn away.

Humans, on the other hand, have completely severed this vital connection. We live almost entirely in our heads, governed by intellectual rules, societal habits, and external trends. Even when our stomach, our tongue, or our sense of smell screams in aversion, "don't eat this!", we override our biological intuition. We force ourselves to consume it because a book told us it was "healthy," or because we do not want to appear impolite at a dinner table. By continuously silencing these quiet physical warning systems, we take our first active steps toward self-destruction.

## The 1/5 Rule – How Overeating Poisons the Body

According to Lazarev's findings, the modern human organism is chronically congested. On average, we consume four times more food than our physical bodies actually require to maintain life and vitality.

To counteract this, Lazarev introduces the 1/5 Rule: ideally, we should consume only one-fifth of the volume of food we currently eat.

When we chronically overeat, the consequences go far beyond weight gain:

**Undigested Food Becomes Poison.** Because the body cannot process such a massive surplus, the undigested food transforms into literal, internal toxins. This toxic overload clogs our digestive tract and pollutes our entire organism.

**The Muting of Our Senses.** This internal poisoning acts like heavy static on a radio. It clogs our intuitive channels, completely dulling our ability to feel what our body actually needs.

**The Loss of Fine Discrimination.** A healthy, clean system instinctively rejects harmful substances. However, a chronically congested human loses this fine sensory discrimination. We become completely blind to the warning signals of our gut, leading us to blindly gobble up whatever is placed in front of us out of pure habit or intellectual conditioning.
