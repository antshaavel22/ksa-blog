## How Does Laser Eye Surgery Work in Seconds?

How is it possible to achieve complete freedom from glasses and microscopic precision in such a short window? The answer lies in space-age engineering: the premium-class SCHWIND AMARIS 1050RS laser, the technological heart of KSA Vision Clinic and the only laser of its kind in Estonia.

In this article, we lift the hood and explain in plain language five marvels of physics and engineering that make Flow3 one of the safest and most precise vision correction procedures in the world.

## The 1050 Hz Speed: Why Faster is Actually Safer

The human eye is a living organ that reacts to its environment. One of the primary risks during laser eye surgery is corneal dehydration (drying of the cornea), which can affect how the laser pulses are absorbed and how the tissue heals. This is where the laser's incredible speed becomes crucial.

The AMARIS 1050RS operates at a repetition rate of 1050 Hz. This means the laser fires an astonishing 1,050 microscopic light pulses every single second! This makes it one of the fastest excimer lasers on the market, cutting the laser ablation time down to a mere 1.3 seconds per diopter.

For the patient, this high-speed performance provides two key benefits:

- **Zero Dehydration.** The cornea does not have time to dry out during the procedure, ensuring highly predictable and accurate treatment results.
- **Less Fatigue.** The time required to focus your gaze on the green fixation light is minimized, making the experience physically and mentally effortless.

## AFLA Algorithm: The Clever 80/20 Rule of Energy Management

If a laser operated at maximum power throughout the entire treatment, the resulting corneal surface would be uneven. If it operated only at a low power setting, the procedure would take far too long. Engineers solved this dilemma with a highly sophisticated algorithm called Automatic Fluence Level Adjustment (AFLA).

AFLA manages the treatment using a classic 80/20 principle:

- **The first 80% of the ablation** is performed at a high energy level. This allows the laser to rapidly and efficiently reshape the bulk of the cornea, keeping the total treatment time as short as possible.
- **The remaining 20% of the treatment** automatically switches to a low energy level. This lower fluence is dedicated to the delicate modeling and final polishing of the corneal surface.

This dual-energy approach results in an exceptionally smooth corneal surface, which is the foundation for rapid and stable visual recovery.

## ITEC Cooling: Preventing Heat Buildup in the Eye

When a laser pulse hits the cornea, it releases energy. Since the laser fires over a thousand times per second, a logical question arises: doesn't the eye heat up?

To prevent any thermal tissue stress, the AMARIS laser incorporates Intelligent Thermal Effect Control (ITEC). ITEC is a dynamic thermal management system that ensures two consecutive laser pulses never hit the same spot on the cornea. The system distributes the pulses across the eye's surface in a mathematically optimized, scattered sequence.

While the laser is active in one area of the eye, previously treated areas are given ample time to cool down completely. Studies conducted with high-speed infrared thermal imaging cameras confirm that corneal warming remains practically negligible during the procedure. This gentle treatment protects delicate ocular tissue and promotes faster postoperative healing.

## Super-Gaussian Beam and 0.54 mm Precision

Imagine trying to paint a microscopic, highly detailed portrait. Would you use a wide house-painting brush or an ultra-fine marker?

The AMARIS laser utilizes Flying-Spot technology with a beam diameter of just 0.54 millimeters—about as fine as the tip of a ballpoint pen. In addition, the beam features a specialized Super-Gaussian profile. This means the laser's energy is not distributed with sharp, abrupt edges, but rather tapers off smoothly toward the periphery.

As these microscopic light spots overlap on the cornea, they create a beautifully smooth, step-free surface. This geometric accuracy ensures maximum corneal uniformity and helps prevent night-vision disturbances, such as glare or halos around light sources.

## SmartPulse and 3D Fullerene: A Digital Twin of the Cornea's Curvature

The human eye is not a flat disc; it is a three-dimensional sphere with complex natural curves. To ensure that the laser understands the exact angle at which every pulse should land on the cornea, the system uses SmartPulse technology.

SmartPulse is built on a mathematical 3D Fullerene model that represents the eye's surface with absolute geometric accuracy. The system calculates the cornea's unique curvature in real time and optimizes the geometric shape of each individual laser pulse to match its target coordinates perfectly.

This technology is particularly vital for touchless surface procedures like Flow3. Because Flow3 does not involve cutting a corneal flap—which in traditional LASIK helps smooth out the underlying tissue as it heals—the laser itself must create an exceptionally smooth surface from the very first second. SmartPulse achieves this, leading to faster healing and sharper vision in the early days following your procedure.
