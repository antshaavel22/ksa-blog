## 5 Reasons for an Online Consultation About Laser Eye Surgery

Life moves fast. We're all looking for ways to care for our health without disrupting our schedule. KSA Silmakeskus, Tallinn's leading laser eye clinic, recognised this. We now offer online consultations for anyone considering vision correction or wanting to understand their eye surgery options better. Here are five reasons why an online consultation might be exactly what you need.

## 1. The smart choice if you wear glasses or contacts

We've been doing this for over 20 years. In a recent survey of 1,500 former glasses wearers, 76% told us the same thing: glasses and contacts cause real problems. Eye strain. Redness.

Dryness. Headaches. The friction of daily life.

If you're between 18 and 45 with a prescription up to -9 diopters, you might be a candidate for the [Flow3 procedure](https://ksa.ee/en/laser-eye-surgery/) — a flapless laser technique that's faster to recover from and safer for active lifestyles.

An online consultation is your chance to get straight answers without the hassle.

## 2. Fast and convenient

No travel. No waiting room. No time off work.

Log in from home with any device that has internet. Within 20 minutes, you'll have spoken to a KSA Silmakeskus specialist and have the answers you came for. That's it. Simple, efficient, and on your schedule.

## 3. High-quality, personalised advice

Our online consultations aren't a shortcut. They're the real thing.

You'll speak with experienced eye specialists — the same ones who perform thousands of procedures each year. They'll answer your specific questions, explain how [laser eye surgery](https://ksa.ee/en/laser-eye-surgery/) or [ICB lens replacement](https://ksa.ee/en/) works, and give you an honest assessment of whether you're a good candidate. If you want to move forward, they'll help you book a full clinical examination at our Tallinn clinic.

## 4. It's completely free

Yes, free. No catch.

In a time when costs are rising, taking advantage of professional advice that doesn't cost anything is smart. Use it to get clarity before committing to anything else.

## 5. Your first step toward clearer vision

An online consultation removes the biggest barrier: not knowing where to start.

After 20 minutes, you'll have a clear picture of your options. You'll understand what's realistic for your eyes. And if you decide to move forward with a procedure, the next steps will feel familiar and straightforward.

Registering for a KSA Silmakeskus online consultation takes less than a minute. Choose a time that works for you, and one of our specialists will meet you there.
